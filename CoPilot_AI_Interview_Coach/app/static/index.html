Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone permission is requested when you enable devices before an interview. Voice transcription depends on browser support and may use an online browser service. Use localhost or a trusted HTTPS origin.

Data and privacy

Interview answers, resumes, transcripts, and reports are saved in your local SQLite database. You can delete individual sessions in Interview History or remove your account and its associated interview data below.

Delete account

This permanently deletes your profile and account-owned interview sessions, transcripts, answers, resumes, and performance reports. This cannot be undone.

Delete your account?

Your profile and all account-owned interview data, including reports and resume text stored in the database, will be permanently deleted. Enter your password to confirm.
